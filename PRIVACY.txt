DYNAMO - USAGE DATA
===================

DYNAMO can send anonymous usage counts. This notice says exactly what leaves the
machine, what never does, and how to switch it off.


WHAT IT IS FOR
--------------

To improve the program: which commands are actually used, on how many targets,
and on which versions of Business Central.

It is not a support channel, it carries no errors and no diagnostics, and
nothing that is sent can be traced back to you, to your company or to the
systems you administer.


WHAT IS SENT
------------

1.  The country, worked out by the receiving service from the network address
    of the connection. The address itself is not stored: the service replaces
    it with 0.0.0.0 as soon as it has read the country from it.

2.  The version of DYNAMO.

3.  Which set of features this copy is running with, and how it was enabled.
    Never the name it is registered to, and never the fingerprint it is tied
    to.

4.  How many on-premises servers and how many online tenants are in the
    list, and how many distinct accounts the on-premises servers are
    reached with. Counts only.

5.  How many tenants sign in with a device code and how many through the
    browser. Counts only.

6.  For every target in the list: whether it is on-premises or online, and for
    online environments whether it is a production or a sandbox environment.

7.  The Business Central version of each target, shortened to major and minor
    only - "27.5", never the full build number.

8.  Which commands have been launched and how many times, each together with
    the kind of target it was launched on and its Business Central version.

9.  An identifier of this installation, plus how long the data waited before
    being sent. The identifier is drawn at random: it is not derived from the
    machine, from the domain or from your account.


WHAT IS NEVER SENT
------------------

Names of servers, service instances, tenants, tenant identifiers, databases,
ports, addresses, accounts, companies; names, identifiers or publishers of
extensions; file paths; the name this copy is registered to and the fingerprint
it is tied to; the labels you give to your tabs; and the text of any error
message - messages coming from Business Central, PowerShell or Windows
routinely contain server names.

Nothing you type is ever sent, and no content of your systems is read for this
purpose.


WHEN IT HAPPENS
---------------

In the background, and never while you wait for anything. The first send
happens shortly after the program starts, and after that not more often than
once every six hours - at most four times a day. If the machine has no way out
to the internet, the attempt fails silently.

Nothing accumulates forever: the oldest counts are dropped.


WHERE THE DATA WAITS
--------------------

In a single readable file, in your user profile:

    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Dynamo\telemetry\telemetry.json

It is plain text: open it with Notepad and you see precisely what is waiting to
be sent. You can delete it at any time, with no consequences for the program.


HOW TO SWITCH IT OFF
--------------------

Tools > Settings > Privacy, clear the checkbox, Save. It stops immediately.


WHO RECEIVES IT
---------------

The data is received by Microsoft Azure Application Insights, in the West
Europe region, and is kept for 90 days.

The controller is Vito Iacovone, the author of DYNAMO. For any request about
this notice, use the same channel through which you obtained the program.
